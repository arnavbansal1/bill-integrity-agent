    Architecture · process flow · prototype vs production · path to production

    Choice: a bounded agentic workflow. The model extracts evidence (one call, quotes only); deterministic code owns validation and pricing; a biller approves anything that moves money. The steps are fixed and the risk is money, so autonomy stays narrow.

    How it works and what we learned

    Three layers. 1) The AI only reads documents and returns evidence with exact quotes. 2) Fixed rules do everything that touches money: contract version by pickup date, fuel surcharge week, density class, freight-all-kinds (FAK) ranges, accessorial prices. 3) A biller approves, overrides or escalates every bill.

    Safety does not depend on the model. Every quote is checked word for word against the documents; an unverified quote sends the bill to a person. The model's confidence is ignored for status. Backstop rules re-read the bill of lading's requested services and every pickup date themselves, so a miss by the AI still lands in review, never in a silent charge.

    v2 backstops (added after the blind test). Rules, not the AI, now also: scan delivery notes for any service or unsupported charge the AI did not extract; flag documents that try to instruct the system; refuse a charge unless a quote names the service; check negation only in the clause that names the service; verify inspection numbers as whole numbers in order; escalate certificates with pieces of different sizes. Each can only send a bill to a person, never add a charge.

    Source-of-truth precedence. Contract / tariff and shipment evidence (BOL, POD, W&I) > operational notes and free text > model inference. Biller resolutions become candidate memory. Three examples marks a pattern, not activation: rules activate by risk tier (routing-only rules after review, regression and scoping; customer-specific money rules also need more examples and a short shadow; general money rules need the most evidence, wider regression, owner approval and shadow validation). A learned rule never overrides contract or shipment evidence.

    Outcomes. Clear (no change) · Propose fix (evidence complete; biller approves) · Request info (a document is missing) · Escalate (conflict or ambiguity a person must decide).
